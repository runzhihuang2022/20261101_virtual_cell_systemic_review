Figure 4. Tissue contexts for AI virtual-cell applications

Ten tissues provide distinct repair contexts: skin [91, 166], lung [92], kidney [93], heart [27, 94], liver [95], intestine [242], muscle [247], neural tissue [298], bone marrow [205] and eye [225]. Mechanistic models describe closure, remodelling, tissue architecture and population dynamics. Geneformer contributes learned regulatory target selection and cardiac microtissue testing [27]. The neural study concerns axonal growth in zebrafish larvae, and the ocular study concerns corneal epithelium. These contexts define tissue-level endpoints for connecting a predicted cellular response with repair.
