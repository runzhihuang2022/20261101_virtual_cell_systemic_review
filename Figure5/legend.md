Figure 5. From predicted cell states to repair outcomes

A proposed development pathway for repair-oriented AI virtual cells. (A) Define the cellular prediction and the donor, perturbation or tissue transfer being evaluated. (B) Compare predictions with simple baselines and positive controls using calibrated, perturbation-specific metrics [145, 146, 147, 148]. (C) Measure molecular response, cell behaviour and tissue function in a linked experimental design. (D) Extend the study to external replication, prospective testing and a defined clinical use [1]. The arrows organise the successive evaluation tasks.

Alt text: A four-stage pathway connects prediction-task definition, computational comparison, molecular-to-tissue experiments and prospective application.
