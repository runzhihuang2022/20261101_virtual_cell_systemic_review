Figure 3. Cell fate and engineered environments

(A) CellOracle combines transcriptomic and regulatory information to predict TF-dependent state transitions, with selected developmental predictions tested experimentally [20]. (B) Hyaluronic-acid matrix experiments show that increased stress relaxation favours neural differentiation in three-dimensional culture and suppresses it in two-dimensional culture [235]. (C) Squidiff connects conditional transcriptomic prediction with human blood-vessel organoids exposed to neutron irradiation and G-CSF [84]. (D) A proposed skin study connects donor-aware prediction, perturbation experiments, functional readouts and model refinement. G-CSF, granulocyte colony-stimulating factor; HA, hyaluronic acid.

Alt text: Regulatory prediction, matrix-dependent neural fate and vascular-organoid perturbation experiments inform a proposed prediction–experiment cycle for skin.
