Figure 1. AI virtual-cell components and supporting methods

The map groups 20 selected studies by computational role. (A) Regulatory and cell-fate inference [20, 30, 31, 32, 86, 87]. (B) Conditional perturbation-response prediction [8, 19, 21, 82, 83, 84]. (C) Cellular representation and molecular or morphological prediction [7, 33, 34]. (D) Spatial and multicellular context [9, 35, 36]. (E) Tahoe-100M supplies experimentally measured drug responses [10]. (F) scPerturBench provides a framework for evaluating perturbation predictions [145]. Explicit mechanistic and multiscale models contribute complementary physiological and physical descriptions [1, 4, 11]. GRN, gene regulatory network; TF, transcription factor; ODE, ordinary differential equation; ABM, agent-based model.

Alt text: Six groups of methods and resources surround a cell, linking regulation, perturbation responses, molecular readouts and neighbouring cells.
