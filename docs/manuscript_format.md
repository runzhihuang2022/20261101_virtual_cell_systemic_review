# Manuscript structure

Verified against the Burns & Trauma author instructions and the reference article https://doi.org/10.1093/burnst/tkag019 on 1 November 2026.

The Review uses Abstract, Background, Review, Conclusions and Declarations as major headings. The duplicated Introduction heading was removed. Literature search and evidence assessment is a second-level subsection of Background; the original nine review topics are second-level headings under Review, with their subdivisions one level below. Font settings are specified in config/journal_format.json as manuscript formatting choices; the journal does not prescribe these exact point sizes. Figure 4 is cited in Section 8.1 and figures first appear in order 1 to 5. The repository is cited in Availability of data and materials as reference 354, following the 353 retained research references.
